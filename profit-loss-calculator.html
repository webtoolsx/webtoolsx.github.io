How to use

A Profit and Loss Calculator helps calculate the outcome of a transaction based on input values like buy price, sell price, quantity, and brokerage fees.

  • Input Fields: Users fill in the buy price, sell price, quantity, and brokerage percentage or flat fee.
  • Brokerage Type: Users select between percentage-based or flat fee brokerage.
  • Calculate Button: Processes inputs to calculate net profit or loss instantly.

What is a Profit and Loss Calculator?

A Profit and Loss Calculator is a financial tool used to determine the profitability of a stock transaction or investment by subtracting cost price and brokerage from sell price.

When to Use a Profit and Loss Calculator?

  • Before making stock or crypto investments.
  • For evaluating trading strategy profitability.
  • To calculate net gains after brokerage costs.

Formula for Profit and Loss

Profit: Profit occurs when your sell price exceeds buy price and brokerage expenses.

Loss: Loss occurs when your total costs exceed revenue.

Profit = (Sell Price * Quantity) - (Buy Price * Quantity) - Brokerage
Loss = (Buy Price * Quantity) + Brokerage - (Sell Price * Quantity)
