Age Calculation Method
Age = Reference Date − Date of Birth
Subtract birth year from current year

Years = Current Year − Birth Year (raw count).

Adjust for month & day

If today's month/day hasn't passed the birth month/day yet this year, subtract 1 from years.

Handle month carry-over

If current day < birth day, borrow days from the previous month for accurate day count.

Example

Born May 15, 2000 → Today June 10, 2026 = 26 years, 0 months, 26 days.
