Our free Percentage Change Calculator helps you instantly compute the percent increase or decrease between any two values. Whether you're tracking stock price changes, comparing sales growth, analyzing revenue trends, or measuring weight loss progress — this tool delivers fast, accurate results using the standard percentage change formula.

2

Enter the Initial Value

Type your starting value in the "Initial Value" field. For example, the original price of a product, a stock's opening price, or last month's revenue figure.

3

Percentage Change Formula
% Change = (Final − Initial)Initial × 100
Identify your two values

Initial Value (X1) — the starting number (e.g., stock price on Day 1). Final Value (X2) — the ending number (e.g., stock price today).

Subtract: Final − Initial

Find the raw change. A positive result = increase. A negative result = decrease.

Change = X2 − X1
Divide by Initial Value

Get the relative (proportional) change so you can compare values of any size.

Relative Change = (X2 − X1) / X1
Multiply by 100 to get %

Convert the decimal to a human-readable percentage.

% Change = Relative Change × 100
Example: A stock goes from $100 → $135.
% Change = ((135 − 100) / 100) × 100 = +35% (increase)
Example: Revenue drops from $5,000 → $4,000.
% Change = ((4000 − 5000) / 5000) × 100 = −20% (decrease)

Percentage change measures how much a quantity has increased or decreased relative to its original value, expressed as a percentage. A positive result means growth; a negative result means a decline. The formula is: % Change = ((New − Old) / Old) × 100.

% Change tells you how much a value grew or shrank relative to the starting point (e.g., "sales increased 20%"). % Of tells you what fraction of one value another is (e.g., "the final value is 120% of the initial value"). Use % Change for trend analysis and % Of for ratio analysis.
