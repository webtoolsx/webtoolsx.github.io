About Greenwich Mean Time (GMT)

Greenwich Mean Time (GMT) is the mean solar time at the Royal Observatory in Greenwich, London. GMT is UTC+0 and is used as standard time in the UK, Ireland, and several West African nations during winter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is GMT time now?

GMT stands for Greenwich Mean Time (UTC+0). You can check the current live GMT clock display on this page.

Does GMT change in summer in London?

During summer, the UK switches from GMT (UTC+0) to British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1).

What is the difference between GMT and UTC?

GMT is a time zone based on solar time at Greenwich. UTC is a high-precision time standard based on atomic clocks. In practice, GMT and UTC show the exact same time.
