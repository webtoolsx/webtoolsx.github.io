Profit & Loss Formula
P/L = (Sell − Buy) × Qty − Brokerage
Gross Profit / Loss

Gross P/L = (Sell - Buy) × Quantity

Subtract Brokerage Cost

Net P/L = Gross P/L − Brokerage Amount

Return on Investment (%)

ROI % = (Net P/L / Investment) × 100

Example

Buy 100 @ ₹200, Sell @ ₹240, Brokerage ₹50 → Net = ₹3,950

Profit = (Sell Price - Buy Price) × Quantity - Brokerage. Positive = profit; negative = loss.

Brokerage is the fee charged by your stockbroker for executing buy and sell orders — either flat per order or a percentage of trade value.

Gross profit = revenue minus cost. Net profit = gross profit minus all expenses including brokerage and taxes.

ROI = (Net Profit / Total Investment) × 100. For a trade: (Net Profit / (Buy Price × Quantity)) × 100.

The result shows a negative value — a loss equal to (Buy - Sell) × Quantity + Brokerage.
