Our free Loan EMI Calculator helps you instantly compute your monthly EMI (Equated Monthly Installment), total interest payable, and total repayment amount. Use it for home loans, car loans, personal loans, or any fixed-rate loan — no spreadsheet needed.

1

Enter the Loan Amount

Type the principal amount you wish to borrow (e.g., ₹5,00,000 or $10,000).

2

Enter the Annual Interest Rate

Type your loan's annual interest rate as a percentage (e.g., 8.5). Monthly rate is computed automatically.

3

Enter the Loan Tenure

Set the repayment period in months (e.g., 60 for 5 years or 240 for 20 years).

4

Click Calculate

Hit Calculate to instantly see your monthly EMI, total interest paid, and total payment.

5

Review the Breakdown

Use the result panel and chart to compare the principal vs interest split and plan affordability.

6

Reset & Try Scenarios

Hit Reset and try different amounts, tenures, or rates to compare EMI scenarios before finalising.

EMI Formula
EMI = [P × R × (1+R)ⁿ] ÷ [(1+R)ⁿ − 1]
P = Principal Loan Amount

The total amount borrowed (e.g., ₹5,00,000).

R = Monthly Interest Rate

Annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100. For 8.4% annual: R = 8.4 / 12 / 100 = 0.007

N = Loan Tenure in Months

For a 5-year loan: N = 5 × 12 = 60 months

Example

P=₹5L, R=8.4%p.a., N=60 months → EMI ≈ ₹10,224/month

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is a fixed monthly payment to repay a loan over a set period. Each payment covers both principal repayment and interest charges.

Even a 0.5% difference in interest rate can significantly change your EMI and total interest over a long-term loan. Always compare rates across lenders.

A pre-payment is an extra lump-sum towards the principal, which either lowers future EMIs or shortens the tenure depending on your bank's policy.

Yes, the standard EMI formula applies to any fixed-rate loan — home, car, personal, or business. Floating rate loans have variable EMIs as the rate changes.

Lower your EMI by: (1) Negotiating a lower rate, (2) Increasing tenure, (3) Making a larger down payment, or (4) Pre-paying when possible.
